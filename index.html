Frequently Asked Questions

Is the camera recording me?

No. All image processing happens locally on your mirror’s hardware. Nothing is uploaded to cloud servers, and a physical shutter is included.

Are there monthly subscription fees?

Never. EVA is a one-time hardware purchase with lifetime mobile app access.

How does the mirror connect to my phone?

EVA syncs via encrypted local Wi-Fi and Bluetooth to your companion mobile app.

On-Device Privacy Policy

Images are processed locally on the mirror. Nothing is uploaded to cloud servers without your permission.

A physical magnetic shutter lets you switch the camera off, and Manual Mode works with no camera at all.

Your data stays yours, and it is never used to train or improve our product.
